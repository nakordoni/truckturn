# TruckTurn: HGV Navigator

### Navigation made for professional truck drivers.

![TruckTurn](https://nakordoni.eu/truckturn/store/icon_512.png)

TruckTurn plans the route for your actual vehicle, shows what is ahead on the road and connects you with other drivers. Android first; iOS is in development.

![soon](https://img.shields.io/badge/Coming%20soon%20to%20Google%20Play-555555?style=for-the-badge&logo=googleplay&logoColor=white)

## Features

- **Routes for your truck**: height, weight, width, length, axle load and ADR
- **Route preview** with alternatives, toll estimate and an avoid-tolls option
- **Driving bans on your route** and height, weight and width limits
- **Traffic incidents and weather** along the way
- **Voice guidance** with lane hints and the speed limit
- **Live border queues** with the truck lane shown separately
- **Truck parking, fuel with AdBlue, weigh stations and truck washes** ahead of you
- **Driving-time countdown** to your next break (estimates only)
- **Safe driving mode** with large, simple controls on the move
- **Driver Connect**: end-to-end encrypted chat and voice messages
- **Dashcam** with loop recording; clips stay on your phone
- Day and night map, portrait and landscape
